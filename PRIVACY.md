# Privacy notice

This app is the field client for [fpo-parakh](https://github.com/intellecap-weia-fpo/fpo-parakh). The [server's privacy notice](https://github.com/intellecap-weia-fpo/fpo-parakh/blob/main/PRIVACY.md) covers the data, your rights and how to reach the organisation running the server.

It keeps assessment data on the device until it syncs. Voice input, if you choose to use it, relies on the phone's own speech recognition, so Apple or Google may process the audio depending on the device and settings. Document pre-fill sends the documents you choose to the server.
